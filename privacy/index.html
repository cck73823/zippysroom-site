Zippy's Room – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Zippy's Room: Learn & Play ("the app") is a free learning game for young children, made by an independent parent developer. We take children's privacy seriously, so the app is built to collect nothing.

No data collection

  • The app does not collect, store on any server, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.
  • The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.
  • The app works fully offline and does not request internet access.

Data kept on the device

Game progress, coins, settings and the daily play-time limit are saved on your device so your child can continue where they left off. We never receive this data. If you have turned on your phone's own backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone/iPad), the operating system may include this app data in your backup so it can be restored on a new device; that backup lives in your own Google or Apple account and is governed by their privacy policies. Uninstalling the app deletes the data from the device.

Feedback and beta sign-up (optional, outside the app)

Parents can choose to send us feedback through a form or by email, or sign up to test pre-release versions. This is always voluntary and only reachable by a parent (behind the parental gate in the app, or on this website). What you choose to send (for example your message, device model, app version, your child's age, and optionally your email address) is used only to reply to you, fix problems and improve the app. Form responses are stored in Google Forms/Google Sheets and emails in our Gmail account; we do not sell or share them. You can ask us to delete anything you sent at any time by emailing us.

Device features

On Android the app uses vibration for gentle feedback. It does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children ages 3–8 and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR, the Google Play Families Policy and the Apple App Store Kids Category guidelines. The app itself collects no personal information. We do not knowingly collect information from children; feedback and beta sign-ups are intended for parents only. Parent settings are protected by a parental gate.

Third parties

No third-party SDKs that collect data are included. The app uses open-source components (Capacitor, Fredoka font) that do not collect data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
