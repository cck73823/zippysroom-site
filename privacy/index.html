Zippy's Room – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Zippy's Room: Learn & Play ("the app") is a free learning game for young children, made by an independent parent developer. We take children's privacy seriously, so the app is built to collect nothing.

No data collection

  • The app does not collect, store on any server, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.
  • The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.
  • The app works fully offline and does not request internet access.

Data kept on the device

Game progress, coins, settings and the daily play-time limit are saved only on your device so your child can continue where they left off. This data never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Device features

On Android the app uses vibration for gentle feedback. It does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children ages 3–8 and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR, the Google Play Families Policy and the Apple App Store Kids Category guidelines. Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing to review, export or delete on our side. Parent settings are protected by a parental gate.

Third parties

No third-party SDKs that collect data are included. The app uses open-source components (Capacitor, Fredoka font) that do not collect data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
