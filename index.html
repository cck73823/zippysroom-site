Zippy's Room: Learn & Play

Learn first, then play

The learning content follows what US kindergartens teach: lowercase and uppercase letters, phonics, early reading and spelling, and numbers to 100. Kids earn coins by finishing learning games, then spend them on board games, snacks for Zippy and fun surprises in Zippy's room. Every game adapts to your child: it gets a little harder after a few correct answers and gentler after a miss.

Read

Letter tracing (lowercase & uppercase), clapping syllables, beginning, middle and ending sounds, rhymes and first spelling.

Math

Counting, addition, take away, make 10, teen numbers and a number line up to 100.

Think

Patterns, memory match, simple coding with arrows, rotating shapes and odd one out.

Play

Tic-tac-toe, a color card game, five in a row, reversi, pegs, chess, Chinese chess and Chinese dark chess, each with kid-friendly levels.

A look inside

Built for parents, too

  • Healthy screen time: a calm visual timer, short sessions and a gentle “Zippy is sleepy” ending. No streaks, no nagging, no endless loops.
  • You set the limits: choose how long each play session lasts (up to 20 minutes, 10 by default) and, if you like, a maximum play time per day. After a session ends, a new one starts only after a 30-minute break. Need a little more time? Unlock extra minutes from the parent area.
  • Parent area behind a lock: session length and daily time limit, sound and haptics, and a Progress page showing what your child is learning.
  • Private by design: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no internet needed. Progress stays on the device and in your own phone backup.
  • Great for travel: plays fully offline, on flights or while waiting for food.
  • Works on phones, foldables and tablets. Voices are AI-generated.
